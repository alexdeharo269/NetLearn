## Draft captions

**fig_cii_orders. Subgraph centrality is reproduced by the leading orders of its walk expansion.** (a) Subgraph centrality $C_{ii}=(e^{\tilde W})_{ii}$ against its order-2 truncation $1+\tilde s_i^2Y_i/2$ for the 13,455 nodes of 150 random connectomes, coloured by degree; dashed line, identity ($R^2=0.9993$; with order 3, 0.9997). (b) Fraction of $C_{ii}-1$ reproduced by the expansion truncated at orders 2, 3 and 4, averaged over all nodes of the 3,901 connectomes in degree bins.

**fig_memory_predictors. Memory follows the order-2 term summed over the network, the stable rank.** (a) MC against the stable rank $r_s=\sum_i\tilde s_i^2Y_i/0.9801$, 3,901 connectomes coloured by cohort. (b) Correlation of MC with each single walk order, $\log\mathrm{Tr}\,\tilde W^n$ (bars; $n=2$ is the stable rank), and with $\langle C_{ii}\rangle$ truncated at order $n$ (line). (c) Predictors ordered from the topology, through the walk expansion, to the whole linear response and the spectrum. Bars, pooled correlation with MC; line and dot, range and median within the nine cohorts; diamonds, partial correlation given the stable rank (cubic in $\log r_s$). $G=\langle\tilde s^2\rangle$ and $L$ are the strength and disparity factors of the exact factorisation $0.9801\,r_s=N\,G\,D\,L$; linear attenuation $(I-\alpha\tilde W)^{-1}$ and SAR covariance $(I-\alpha\tilde W)^{-2}$ with $\alpha=0.5$ (Fakhar et al. 2025); average controllability, the trace of the controllability Gramian (Gu et al. 2015); eigenvalue entropy, the structural complexity of Fakhar et al. 2026. (d) Share of the across-connectome variance of $\log r_s$ carried by $G$, $D$ and $L$, over all connectomes and within each cohort. (e) Correlation with MC of the SAR covariance as the coupling $\alpha$ approaches the critical value, and of the Gramian of the reservoir itself (average controllability). (f) Change in MC of five null models with respect to the real connectome, 100 age-balanced connectomes (stochastic nulls averaged over 10 realisations; deterministic nulls over 10 input realisations); diamonds, median change predicted from the change in stable rank.

**fig_lifespan_aging. Weight disparity, interhemispheric weight and memory across the lifespan.** As v3 Fig. 3 (panels a and b from v1 Fig. 4), with the virtual aging in (h) simulated.

**figS_development. Development, within cohorts.** (a) Correlation with age, within each cohort (95% CI), of MC and of the homotopic share $h$. (b) MC against age within camCAN (single site), with 5-year bin means (95% CI) and the turning point of a piecewise-linear fit (35 years; bootstrap 95% CI 30–55, shaded). (c, d) MC and $h$ of HCPya and camCAN participants of the same ages (22–37 years).

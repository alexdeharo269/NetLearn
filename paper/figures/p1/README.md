## Captions

**Fig. 3. Lifespan reorganisation of weight disparity and its structural correlates.** Top, against degree; bottom, across the lifespan. (a) Nodal disparity $\Upsilon=kY$ (points, nodes of a random subsample of connectomes), null-model mean and $\pm2\sigma$ envelope limited to $1\le\Upsilon\le k$ (grey), fully heterogeneous limit $\Upsilon=k$ (dashed red) and degree-binned mean over all connectomes, $\propto k^{0.35}$ (blue). (b) Share of $C_{ii}-1$ captured by the walk expansion truncated at orders 2 to 5 (mean over nodes; interquartile range for orders 2 and 2-3). (c) Share of a region's squared weight carried by its homotopic connection, $h_i=w_{ii'}^2/\sum_jw_{ij}^2$ (mean and 95% CI over nodes), against the $1/k$ expected if the weights of a region were exchangeable. (d-f) Per-age means of each cohort of the disparity ratio, subgraph centrality and homotopic share $h$.

**Fig. 5. Reservoir memory across the lifespan and its structural predictors.** (a) MC against age (grey points) with a linear fit in development (age at most 32 years, the minimum of the running mean of MC) and in aging; dashed red, linear fits of the disparity ratio (right axis). (b) MC against the stable rank $r_s$, by regime. (c) Correlation with MC in development and aging of the stable rank, subgraph centrality, communicability, the disparity ratio, strength, degree and average controllability (trace of the controllability Gramian). (d) Partial correlation with MC given the stable rank (cubic in $\log r_s$), all connectomes. (e) Change in MC of five null models with respect to the real connectome, 100 age-balanced connectomes: uniform weights, broken-stick weights, weights reshuffled among the connections (10 realisations each), interhemispheric weights halved, and interhemispheric weight fraction set to the pooled mean, 0.29 (both over 10 input realisations); diamonds, median change predicted from the change in stable rank.

**Fig. 6. Closed walks beyond the stable rank, and the strongest connection across the lifespan.** (a) Correlation with MC of closed walks of length $n$ ($\log\mathrm{Tr}\,\tilde W^n$, all connectomes; filled, $n=2$ is the stable rank) and partial correlation given the stable rank (open; cubic in $\log r_s$). Right, a closed walk of even length can go back and forth along the strongest connection; one of odd length must close a triangle or another odd cycle. Given the stable rank, odd lengths add and even lengths subtract. (b) MC against the homotopic share $h$, all connectomes coloured by age; large points, means of newborns, 21-37 years and 75 years or more. Brains, the strongest connection between the hemispheres (green) and within a hemisphere (brown) at each stage, line width proportional to its mean share of the squared weight.

Colours: blue, disparity, order 2 and even walks; orange, triangles and odd walks; green, interhemispheric; brown, intrahemispheric; red, disparity ratio; grey, null models; yellow and purple, development and aging.

**figS_development. Development, within cohorts.** (a) Correlation with age, within each cohort (95% CI), of MC and of $h$. (b) MC against age within camCAN (single site), with 5-year bin means (95% CI) and the turning point of a piecewise-linear fit (35 years; bootstrap 95% CI 30-55, shaded). (c, d) MC and $h$ of HCPya and camCAN participants of the same ages (22-37 years).

## Captions

**Fig. 3. Lifespan reorganisation of weight disparity and its structural correlates.** (a) Nodal disparity $\Upsilon(k)=kY$ against degree $k$ (points, nodes of a random subsample of connectomes). Grey line and band, null-model mean $\mu(\Upsilon_{null})$ and its $\pm2\sigma$ envelope, limited to $1\le\Upsilon\le k$; dashed red line, fully heterogeneous limit $\Upsilon=k$; solid blue curve, degree-binned mean over all connectomes, $\langle\Upsilon\rangle_k\propto k^{0.35}$. (b) Share of $C_{ii}-1$ captured by the walk expansion truncated at orders 2, 3 and 4, by degree (mean over nodes); shaded, the contribution of the order-3 term (weighted triangles); legend, $R^2$ with $C_{ii}$ over all nodes. (c-e) Lifespan trajectories, averaged over subjects per age and coloured by cohort: (c) disparity ratio $\langle\Upsilon/\Upsilon_{null}\rangle$, (d) subgraph centrality $\langle C_{ii}\rangle$, (e) homotopic share $h$ of the squared weight.

**Fig. 5. Reservoir memory across the lifespan and its structural predictors.** (a) MC against age (grey points) with a linear fit in development (age at most 32 years) and aging; dashed red line, disparity ratio (right axis). (b) MC against the stable rank $r_s$, by regime. (c) Correlation with MC in development and aging of the stable rank (order-2 term summed over nodes), the weighted triangles (order 3), subgraph centrality, communicability, the disparity ratio, strength, degree and average controllability (trace of the controllability Gramian). (d) Partial correlation with MC given the stable rank (cubic in $\log r_s$), all connectomes. (e) Change in MC of five null models with respect to the real connectome, 100 age-balanced connectomes (stochastic nulls averaged over 10 realisations, deterministic nulls over 10 input realisations); diamonds, median change predicted from the change in stable rank.

**Fig. 6. Interhemispheric weight and the aging course of memory.** camCAN and HCPa, age > 32 years, pooled. (a) MC, subgraph centrality, disparity ratio and homotopic share $h$, standardised within each cohort, in 5-year bins (mean and 95% CI). (b) MC against $h$, coloured by age. (c) MC observed in each decade (mean and 95% CI) and MC of the connectomes aged 33-44 years when their interhemispheric weights are rescaled to the interhemispheric weight fraction of each decade (simulated).

**figS_development. Development, within cohorts.** (a) Correlation with age, within each cohort (95% CI), of MC and of $h$. (b) MC against age within camCAN (single site), with 5-year bin means (95% CI) and the turning point of a piecewise-linear fit (35 years; bootstrap 95% CI 30-55, shaded). (c, d) MC and $h$ of HCPya and camCAN participants of the same ages (22-37 years).

## Captions

**Fig. 3. Lifespan reorganisation of weight disparity and its structural correlates.** Top, against degree; bottom, across the lifespan. (a) Nodal disparity $\Upsilon=kY$ (points, nodes of a random subsample of connectomes), null-model mean and $\pm2\sigma$ envelope limited to $1\le\Upsilon\le k$ (grey), fully heterogeneous limit $\Upsilon=k$ (dashed red) and degree-binned mean over all connectomes, $\propto k^{0.35}$ (blue). (b) Share of $C_{ii}-1$ captured by the walk expansion truncated at orders 2 to 5 (mean over nodes; interquartile range for orders 2 and 2-3). (c) Share of a region's squared weight carried by its homotopic connection, $h_i=w_{ii'}^2/\sum_jw_{ij}^2$ (mean and 95% CI over nodes), against the $1/k$ expected if the weights of a region were exchangeable. (d-f) Per-age means of each cohort of the disparity ratio, subgraph centrality and homotopic share $h$.

**Fig. 5. Reservoir memory across the lifespan and its structural predictors.** (a) MC against age (grey points) with a linear fit in development (age at most 32 years, the minimum of the running mean of MC) and in aging; dashed red, linear fits of the disparity ratio (right axis). (b) MC against the stable rank $r_s$, by regime. (c) Correlation with MC in development and aging of the stable rank (order-2 term summed over nodes), the weighted triangles (order 3), subgraph centrality, communicability, the disparity ratio, strength, degree and average controllability (trace of the controllability Gramian). (d) Partial correlation with MC given the stable rank (cubic in $\log r_s$), all connectomes. (e) Change in MC of four null models with respect to the real connectome, 100 age-balanced connectomes (stochastic nulls averaged over 10 realisations, the interhemispheric one over 10 input realisations); diamonds, median change predicted from the change in stable rank.

**Fig. 6. Interhemispheric weight, walk lengths and the strongest connections.** (a) MC against the homotopic share $h$, all connectomes, coloured by age. (b) Closed walks of length $n$ ($\log\mathrm{Tr}\,\tilde W^n$): correlation with MC (filled) and partial correlation given the stable rank (open); even lengths blue, odd lengths orange. (c) Share of the squared weight carried by the strongest connection between the hemispheres and by the strongest connection within a hemisphere, coloured by age; dotted line, equal shares.

Colours: blue, disparity and order 2; orange, triangles and odd walks; green, interhemispheric; red, disparity ratio; grey, null models; yellow and purple, development and aging.

**figS_development. Development, within cohorts.** (a) Correlation with age, within each cohort (95% CI), of MC and of $h$. (b) MC against age within camCAN (single site), with 5-year bin means (95% CI) and the turning point of a piecewise-linear fit (35 years; bootstrap 95% CI 30-55, shaded). (c, d) MC and $h$ of HCPya and camCAN participants of the same ages (22-37 years).

## Numbers (`paper/data/p1/numbers_p1.json`)

- Walk expansion of $C_{ii}$, $R^2$ over all nodes: $Y_i$ 0.006; $\tilde s_i^2$ 0.71; order 2 0.9993; orders 2-3 0.9997. Order 3 adds 1-2% of $C_{ii}-1$ below degree 10 and about 6% above degree 40.
- $\langle\Upsilon\rangle_k$ above the null mean for $k\ge4$ and above its $2\sigma$ envelope for $k\ge12$; exponent 0.35.
- Homotopic connection: 19% of a region's squared weight at degrees 18-45, against 4% for exchangeable weights.
- MC vs log stable rank: $r=0.95$ (development 0.95, aging 0.93; within cohorts median 0.89, 0.82-0.94). Given the stable rank, age no longer predicts MC (development -0.04, aging -0.03).
- Nulls (median change, observed / predicted): Uniform -1.89 / -1.94; Broken stick -0.79 / -1.17; Reshuffle +0.05 / +0.08; Interhemispheric x1/2 +1.07 / +1.05.
- MC vs h: $r=-0.70$ (development -0.75, aging -0.65).
- The strongest interhemispheric connection carries 5% of the squared weight in newborns, 36% at 21-37 years and 7% after 75; the strongest intrahemispheric one 8.5%, 3% and 12%. Within camCAN and HCPa the first decreases with age (r = -0.49, -0.45) and the second increases (0.39, 0.46). r(MC, strongest interhemispheric share) = -0.83.
- Interhemispheric weight equalised (simulated): correlation of MC with age within camCAN 0.36 -> 0.09, within HCPa 0.22 -> 0.09; developmental cohorts near 0 before and after. Equalising only the homotopic connections does not remove it (0.26, 0.28).
- With age, the strongest intrahemispheric connection (superior-middle frontal, SMA-putamen, postcentral-putamen) gains share of the squared weight (r = 0.37 camCAN, 0.46 HCPa; 5.4% at 33-44 years, 12.1% at 75-90).
